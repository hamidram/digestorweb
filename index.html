Help

Basics. Pick a chip in each row to narrow the list. Tap a headline to jump to its brief. “Deep dive” opens more detail; “Read source” opens the original story in a new tab.

Rate stories with the stars. Ratings, clicks and deep dives suggest keywords on the Learn page.

Advanced. Add keywords in Settings, then choose “My Digest”. “Surprise me” picks one random option in every row. Translated text is machine-made; use “Show original” to compare.

About

TopicsDigester collects news from sources you can see, groups stories about the same event, summarises them and lets you read or listen. Finite digest, no ads, nothing unsolicited, several sides, quiet by default, private by default, your language.

Summaries and translations are machine-made and may contain mistakes. Every story links to its source. News belongs to its publishers.

Privacy

No accounts. Your keywords, ratings and settings stay in this browser and are never sent to our server. The server only holds public news and cached summaries. Draft text: have it reviewed before launch.
