Help

1. Read the digest
  1. Tap a headline to jump to its brief. “Deep dive” opens more detail; “Read source” opens the original story in a new tab.
  2. Rate a story with the stars. Ratings, clicks and deep dives suggest keywords on the Learn page.
  3. Press Refresh to reload. The status line shows progress and any problem.
2. Use the filters
  • Scope: My Digest (only stories matching your keywords), All, a region (Local (WA/AU), US, Europe, Asia, Global), the topic scopes Middle East and Iran (stories from any outlet), or a language: English or Persian.
  • Field: Economy, Politics, Geopolitics, History, Tech, Social, Comedy, Full movies or Random. Choose Persian + Comedy for Persian comedy, or English + Full movies for English full movies.
  • Type: Everything, Top stories, Popular, Breaking, Opinions or Articles.
  • Comedy, Full movies and the language scopes are word rules on the title, summary and keywords, because stories carry no such tags. They are a good guide, not perfect.
  • Surprise me picks one random choice in every row. Press it again for another.
  • Filters apply to both the stories and the From your YouTube panel.
3. Add your own keywords (any language)
  1. Go to Settings → Keywords, type a word or phrase, such as Australian housing market or سریال ایرانی, then press Add or Enter. Separate several with commas or new lines.
  2. Every word of a phrase must appear in the story or video title, so jimmy kimmel matches more than jimmy kimmel show.
  3. Persian is matched flexibly: the Arabic and Persian forms of the letters ي/ی and ك/ک, half-spaces and accents all count as the same.
  4. Persian keywords appear right to left. Persian text works with both Persian-language stories and translated ones.
  5. Save keywords to text file puts them in the box (and downloads a file). Load from file or Load text below brings them back, for example on another device.
  6. Choose My Digest in the Scope row to see only matching stories and videos.
4. Show videos from your own YouTube
  1. The From your YouTube panel under Headlines shows videos YouTube already recommends to you. They never leave this browser.
  2. Set up once (Chrome on a computer): in Settings press Download the Chrome add-on and unzip it. Open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, press Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder. Pin it with the puzzle-piece icon if you like.
  3. Each time: open youtube.com (home page or search results), scroll a little so videos load, click the add-on icon and press Send. This page opens and the panel fills.
  4. Filters apply to videos too. Videos have no tags, so each choice is a word rule on the title and channel name: regions and topic scopes by place words, languages by script, Field by topic words, Popular means 100,000 views or more, Breaking and Opinions by words such as “breaking” or “analysis”. Matches for your keywords are marked ★ and listed first.
  5. Up to 300 videos are kept. Use Clear saved videos in Settings to start over.
5. Language and audio
  • Text language (Filters panel): English, Persian or Visayan. Stories in another language are translated for you (machine translation, so check the source). Persian shows right to left.
  • Play audio digest reads the top stories aloud with your device’s own voice. Turn it on in Settings → Audio.
6. Something not working?
  • Sample stories only: the news service is not reachable. Press Refresh later.
  • Nothing matches: press Reset filters, or choose a wider Scope.
  • Sent videos but the panel is empty: check the Scope, Field and Type rows, then press Reset filters. The add-on message says how many videos were found; if it found none, scroll YouTube further and try again.
  • The add-on says it cannot find the page: open this site once in Chrome, then press Send again. Videos arrive in the Chrome profile you sent them from.
  • Lost my keywords: browsers can clear site data. Export settings now and then.

About

TopicsDigester collects news from sources you can see, groups stories about the same event, summarises them and lets you read or listen. Finite digest, no ads, nothing unsolicited, several sides, quiet by default, private by default, your language.

Summaries and translations are machine-made and may contain mistakes. Every story links to its source. News belongs to its publishers.

Privacy

Last updated 8 October 2026 · Draft: have it reviewed before launch.

The short version. No accounts, no cookies, no ads, no tracking, no profiles. What you choose and read stays on your device. We only count totals.

What stays on your device only
  • Your keywords, star ratings, which stories you open, your language and audio choices, and any YouTube videos you send with the Chrome add-on.
  • They live in this browser’s own storage and are never sent to our server. We cannot see them, and nobody else can.
  • Use Export settings to keep a copy, or Delete all my data in Settings to erase them. Clearing this site’s data in your browser also erases them.
What our server holds
  • Public news only: story titles, links, summaries and cached translations. Stories are deleted after about 14 days.
  • Daily totals, kept for 90 days with no user ID: how many requests and page loads were made, how many translations were asked for, how many requests were turned away for coming too fast, and how many errors occurred.
  • Country only. To count visits by country, your internet address is looked up in a country list and the country code is counted. The address itself is not saved, not logged and not linked to anything.
  • Errors and news feeds that failed, and how much we spend on AI each day. These contain no visitor information.
What we do not collect
  • Your keywords, ratings, reading history, saved videos, name, email, exact location or device details.
  • We do not use cookies, advertising or analytics services on this page, and we do not sell or share data about you.
Technical details and other parties
  • Your internet address is held only in the server’s memory for about a minute or two so it can limit rapid repeat requests and work out your country. It is never written to our records. Our own request log is switched off.
  • Server error logs may briefly contain technical details, such as for failed connections. They are kept to a fixed size and old entries are deleted automatically.
  • Translations and summaries are made with an AI service (Anthropic). It receives the text of public news stories, never your keywords or anything about you. When you ask for a translation, your browser sends only the story numbers and the language.
  • Our hosting providers (GitHub for this page, a server host for the news service) can keep their own technical logs under their own policies, as can your internet provider.
  • When you open a source story or a YouTube video, that website’s own privacy policy applies.
  • The Chrome add-on reads the titles, channels and links of videos on the YouTube page you choose to send, passes them only to this page in your browser, and stores nothing itself.
Your choices and contact
  • Everything personal is on your device, so you control it: export it, delete it, or simply stop using the site.
  • Questions or takedown requests: use the contact details at feetontheroad.com.au.
  • If we change what we collect, we will update this page and the date above before the change starts.
